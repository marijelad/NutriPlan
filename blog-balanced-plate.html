The balanced plate as a flexible template

Use the plate method as a visual starting point, then adapt portions to appetite and context.

Use the plate method as a visual starting point, then adapt portions to appetite and context.

Use the plate as a picture

Half plants, a quarter protein and a quarter carbohydrate is a useful visual shorthand, not a strict rule.

Adjust for the meal

A bowl, wrap or soup may not look like a plate at all, but the same balance can still be present.

Zoom out to the week

A single meal does not define your overall pattern. Variety and consistency make more sense across several days.
